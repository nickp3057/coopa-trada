Trading rules

Timing
Buying window (buys happen when picks are confirmed)

The automatic run is a practice run unless the server’s .env has both LIVE_TRADING=1 and AUTO_START_LIVE=1. A run you started or stopped yourself that day is left alone.

The bot buys as soon as a pick list is confirmed (Telegram or this dashboard), but only inside this window, counted back from the real close, so half days move it earlier. On a normal day the close is 2:00 PM Mountain.

Exit window (per asset class)

Each endpoint is HH:MM New York time (2 hours ahead of Mountain: 15:35 ET = 1:35 PM MT) or a whole number of minutes before the real session close. On half days, New York times move earlier by as much as the close does.

Standard exits

Example: quoted $0.80 bid / $1.00 ask, bought at $1.00. Measured from the mid, the rules start at $0.90 (take-profit +60% = $1.44, 10-point rule $0.81 before any gain). Measured from the price paid, the position starts at −10% and the 10-point rule would sell it right away. The trade log always shows the real prices paid and received.

Older 10% stops (from before the 10-point rule)

10-point rule (profit-point trailing exit)

Hold logic (take-profit sells today, otherwise hold to the next trading day)

Total budget and ranking

Includes positions held from earlier days and buy orders still working. There is no limit on the number of positions.

Exit repricing

A real sell order that has not filled is canceled and replaced at the current bid (only ever lower, never a market order). After the last attempt you get an alert.

Spending cap

A pick is skipped when its contracts at the ask × 100 cost more than this.

Delta filter

Off by default: Cooper’s paid pick list is assumed to be screened on delta already, so filtering here could discard picks. Bounds are inclusive and compare the absolute delta. Schwab delta uses a 0–1 scale; TVT delta appears to use 0–100, so for TVT both bounds must be above 1 (for example 30 and 80).

Alerts

When something needs a person (an order Schwab may not have received, a sell that failed or isn’t filling, a crash that left something to decide, the Schwab login running out, a run refused or stopped by an error), the app sends a Mac notification and, if set up, a push to your phone through the ntfy app. Alerts never include account numbers or dollar amounts.
